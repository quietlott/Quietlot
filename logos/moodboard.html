Where the ideas come from

Links only. Open them in your browser to see the work. Several are useful for style, not for copying.

    Larkef on Dribbble

    Unused concept called Interlocking 3D Blocks. Closest to the two-block idea in B.

  • Layered monograms
    Pixelcup on Dribbble

    Layered and geometric monograms with a sense of depth.

  • 3D abstract marks
    99designs: abstract 3D logos

    A broad set of abstract 3D marks for comparison.

  • Geometric 3D
    Design.com: 50 3D logos

    Blocks, prisms and cubes that show how depth reads at small sizes.

  • Geometric examples
    Inkbot: 25 geometric logos

    Includes a note on adjusting stroke weight for dark and light modes.

  • Rotating logo
    Framer: Rotating 3D Logo

    Drag to spin, with momentum and slow auto-rotation. Same interaction as our 3D version.

  • Scroll-driven logo
    Motion: scroll-driven logo

    Logo draws in as a wireframe, then fills with reflective material. Good for a landing-page intro.

  • Animated logo
    Meta-style dynamic logo

    A walkthrough of an animated 3D logo in Three.js and Blender.

  • SVG to 3D
    LogRocket: extrude SVG in Three.js

    How to load an SVG with SVGLoader and extrude it. Relevant to how the 3D mark was built.
