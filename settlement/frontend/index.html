Quietlot (prototype)

Off-chain negotiation, on-chain atomic settlement. Agents never hold keys.

Unaudited prototype. Do not use with real funds until the contracts are tested on a testnet and independently audited.

1. Wallet and network

Not connected
Enter the official RPC endpoint for the chain. This prototype does not ship one.

2. Create and sign a mandate

Your wallet signs the mandate (EIP-712). Limits stay in this browser until you share the signed mandate.

Both principals sign the same agreed price and transcript digest. The settlement only goes through if the two mandates match.

Keep your limit private. Share only the signed mandate JSON below with the agent or counterparty you choose.

3. Settle a matched pair

Paste the seller's and buyer's signed mandates (from step 2). The price and transcript come from the signed mandates. The contract checks both signatures, that the two mandates agree, the net floor, and the buyer's cap.

4. Transcript digest and verification

Paste the negotiation transcript. The page computes its digest, which you can copy into step 2. With a settlement transaction hash, the page also compares the digest with the transcriptHash in the Settled event. A match shows the transcript was recorded for that trade. It does not show that the steps followed the rules.
